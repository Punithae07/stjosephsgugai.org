40+ Years

Of shaping futures and building character in Salem.

Anne Marie Javouhey, the Foundress of the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph's of Cluny, answered the divine call earnestly and dedicated her life for the sake of uplifting the downtrodden. Education proved to be the ideal instrument for the purpose and so, the missionary sisters, founded schools and educational institutions in different parts of the world.

St. Joseph (flourished 1st century bc, Nazareth, Galilee, region of Palestine; principal feast day March 19, Feast of St. Joseph the Worker May 1) was, in the New Testament, Jesus' earthly father and the Virgin Mary's husband. St. Joseph is the patron of the universal church in Roman Catholicism, and his life is recorded in the Gospels, particularly Matthew and Luke.
